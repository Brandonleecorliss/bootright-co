Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026
Note from Bootright: This is a plain-language privacy policy written to honestly describe how Bootright handles your information. It is a starting template, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it, especially once real payments are flowing.

Bootright ("Bootright," "we," "us") is a business-launch coaching app operated by Brandon Corliss, based in New Hampshire, USA. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices. Questions: supportbootright@gmail.com.

The short version

We collect the information you give us (your account, your answers, your plans, your money entries) and a little technical data, so the app can work and feel like yours. We don't sell your data. We use a small number of trusted services to run the app, and one of them is an AI provider that processes what you type into the agents. You can delete your account and data anytime.

What we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account info: your email address and password (passwords are handled and encrypted by our authentication provider; we never see your raw password).
  • Your plan and progress: your assessment answers, the business path you pick, the details you enter to personalize your roadmap, roadmap progress, saved budgets, money entries you log, and commitments you write.
  • Email signups: if you join a waitlist or signup form, your email address.
  • Payment info: if you subscribe, our payment processor collects your payment details. We do not store your full card number; we only receive limited billing information (like your subscription status).

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic technical and usage data needed to run and secure the app (such as log data and the authentication tokens stored in your browser to keep you signed in). We keep this minimal.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app: run your assessment, build and personalize your roadmap, save your plans, track your money and commitments.
  • To run the AI agents: when you use an agent feature, the relevant information you provided is sent to our AI provider to generate your response (see below).
  • To manage your account, subscription, and support.
  • To improve the app and keep it secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads or let advertisers pay to influence what Bootright recommends.

The services we use (subprocessors)

Bootright runs on a small set of trusted providers. They process your data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase and Vercel: stores your account, your saved data, and hosts the app.
  • Anthropic: when you use an agent, the information needed for that request (such as your business type, budget, location, and the step or question you're working on) is sent to the AI provider to generate a response. Don't put sensitive personal information into the agent fields.
  • Stripe: processes subscription payments securely.

We may update this list as the app grows. Material changes will be reflected here.

AI features and your inputs

Bootright's agents use AI to generate guidance. What you type into an agent is sent to our AI provider to produce your answer. AI output can be wrong or incomplete, so treat it as a helpful starting point, not gospel, and don't enter information you wouldn't want processed by a third-party AI service.

How long we keep your data

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep limited records (for example, payment records required for tax or legal reasons).

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit much of your information in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and associated data. To request deletion or ask a privacy question, contact supportbootright@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, under GDPR or CCPA) to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your data. We'll honor valid requests.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we can't promise absolute security, but we take it seriously.

Children

Bootright is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Bootright evolves. We'll change the effective date above and, for material changes, give reasonable notice in the app or by email.

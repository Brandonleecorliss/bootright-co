Why this exists

Most business advice isn't wrong. It's just selling you a fantasy.

We're not here to trash people. We're here to bust the patterns that cost real beginners real money. Here's what gets sold, and what's actually true.

"You can start with no money."
Sounds inspiring. Gets people stuck. Most real businesses need a small, honest budget — and knowing the number is what saves you, not pretending it's zero.
"Make money in your first weeks."
The timeline wasn't broken — the promise was. Most businesses take 3–6 months to find steady income. Knowing that keeps you from quitting right before it works.
"Buy my course for the secret."
Most gurus earn from selling the dream, not doing the business. The real "secret" is honest work over honest time. Nobody sells that — it's not exciting.
"A bad month means a bad idea."
Usually false. Most failure is quitting early, skipping validation, or undercharging. The idea is rarely the real problem.
